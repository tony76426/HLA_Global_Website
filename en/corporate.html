PREPARE & UNDERSTAND

What should you prepare before a corporate law consultation?

How do commercial contracts connect with daily operations?

First check the contracting parties, authority, contract version, payment and acceptance milestones, then review how the team actually performs its obligations. Corporate counsel can align transaction terms, internal resolutions and communications so responsibilities remain clear to successors.

What should you review when shareholders disagree?

Review shareholding ratios separately from corporate decision procedures. Gather the articles, shareholder agreements, investments and fund flows, past resolutions and a dispute timeline before considering negotiation, governance changes or dispute resolution.

PRACTICE / HLA

Consider legal risks before the transaction begins.

Corporate legal services go beyond reviewing contracts or litigating after disputes. They help management understand legal consequences in daily decisions, major transactions and governance. HLA advises on shareholder and director relations, governance, investments and partnerships, commercial contracts, receivables, debt, distribution and service arrangements, and operational risks. We assess legal rights, commercial purpose, ability to perform and future dispute mechanisms together, so agreements remain workable when tested.

Businesses often balance speed against risk. Our role is not to slow every decision, but to distinguish acceptable risks from those requiring action. For long-term clients, we can extend support from individual matters to legal workflows, allocation of responsibilities and prevention, integrating legal advice into management.

Key services

  • Corporate governance and shareholders’ / directors’ rights and obligations
  • Investment, collaboration, distribution and commercial contracts
  • Shareholder and corporate control disputes
  • Corporate receivables, security and major debt matters
  • Day-to-day corporate counsel and major decision reviews
  • Commercial negotiation, settlement and dispute prevention

04 / FAQ

Corporate & commercial FAQ

What should be checked first in a commercial contract?＋

Start with signing authority, core obligations, performance dates and risk allocation. Individual terms require assessment against transaction documents.

What documents are useful for the first consultation?＋

Prepare the latest contract, key communications, resolutions or authorization records, and the commercial outcome you seek.
