PROFILE / APPROACH

About Li Hsin-Ting

Attorney Li Hsin-Ting graduated from the Department of Law at National Taipei University and passed both the Taiwan Bar and Judicial Officer examinations. She is the managing partner of HLA Global. Her practice includes cross-border dispute resolution, corporate governance, debt disputes and SPAC-related transactions, integrating business context with legal strategy.

In matters involving transactions and disputes, she first maps the facts and documents, then assesses creditor rights, asset exposure, procedural timing and workable arrangements. Legal arguments and negotiation strategy should be coordinated, with implementation in view.

For day-to-day corporate governance, she emphasizes clear authority, transaction terms and risk allocation. If litigation is required, she uses evidence and procedure to build traceable arguments so clients understand the legal and commercial implications of each option.
