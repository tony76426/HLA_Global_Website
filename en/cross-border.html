PREPARE & UNDERSTAND

What should you prepare before a cross-border consultation?

What connections matter in a cross-border contract?

List the contracting parties, place of performance, location of assets and principal document languages. Check jurisdiction, governing law and service of process. A transaction may raise issues in multiple jurisdictions; separate Taiwan-law work from overseas matters requiring further confirmation.

  • Jurisdiction and Governing Law in Cross-Border Contracts ↗
  • Organizing Transaction Documents and Milestones ↗

How should you prepare for an international commercial dispute?

Prepare complete agreements and exhibits, correspondence and revisions, delivery and payment records, and the locations of the other party and assets. Counsel can assess procedures, communication arrangements and specialist support based on the case rather than a single contractual clause.

PRACTICE / HLA

Build a workable legal approach across jurisdictions and commercial systems.

Cross-border transactions and disputes can be affected simultaneously by different countries’ laws, corporate systems, contract terms and enforcement environments. HLA starts with the transaction structure, identifying parties, shareholdings, funds, performance location, governing law, dispute-resolution provisions and asset location before evaluating possible legal action in Taiwan.

For multinational enterprises, overseas investors and clients doing business abroad, collaboration with external counsel and overseas professionals is important. Matters involving offshore companies, SPACs, cross-border investments, international contracts or foreign assets require coordination across legal systems. Attorney Lee Hsin-Ting’s published experience includes international dispute resolution and SPAC mergers and acquisitions.

  • Cross-border claims and commercial disputes

  • Coordination with overseas professionals and Taiwan proceedings
