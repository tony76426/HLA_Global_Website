What should you do when you receive a police interview notice?

What should you do when you receive a police interview notice?

Preserve the notice and check the recipient’s identity, alleged matter, appearance time and contact person. Prepare documents and an incident timeline. If lawyer attendance is needed, provide the notice and timing when contacting us; specific rights and responses depend on the case.

How should fraud or corporate criminal matters be documented?

Preserve full conversations, fund flows, transaction documents and authority records. Distinguish firsthand facts from information heard from others. Where business transactions or civil claims are involved, document decisions, authorization and performance so criminal proceedings can be coordinated with related disputes.

PRACTICE / HLA

The first priority in a criminal matter is managing procedural and evidentiary risks.

Timing and early decisions can shape the whole criminal case. Police interviews, investigations, searches, seizures, detention, prosecution and trial each have different procedural considerations. HLA helps clients clarify facts, sources of evidence and potentially relevant offense elements, avoiding irreversible statements or decisions under pressure or without sufficient information.

Corporate criminal cases often overlap with governance, money flows, contracts, employee conduct and civil liability. Beyond defense work, we review internal documents, allocation of authority, transaction records and external explanations, coordinating civil or commercial strategy where needed. Effective criminal defense connects procedural rights, admissibility of evidence, fact finding and application of the law.

Key services

  • Defense at police interviews, investigations and criminal trials
  • Fraud, embezzlement, breach of trust and property offenses
  • Defamation, personal data and online criminal matters
  • Corporate executives and internal criminal risks
  • Searches, seizures, detention and procedural safeguards
  • Integrated strategy for overlapping criminal and civil matters
