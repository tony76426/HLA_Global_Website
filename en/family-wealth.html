PREPARE & UNDERSTAND

What should you prepare before a family-law consultation?

How should divorce, parenting and child support be prepared separately?

Divorce procedures, child care and marital property each require different information. Gather existing agreements, care schedules, children’s expenses, assets and debts. Child support calculations and recovery of unpaid support should also be considered separately.

What should you clarify before planning an inheritance or succession?

Prepare a common inventory of heirs, estate assets, liabilities, wills and existing agreements before discussing division or succession. For co-owned real estate, check registration and use so family negotiations can lead to appropriate documents and implementation.

PRACTICE / HLA

Resolve legal relationships while understanding family structure and future needs.

Family cases involve law, emotions, property and relationships and require a more sensitive approach than most business disputes. HLA assists with divorce, parental rights and contact, child support, division of marital assets, protection orders, inheritance, wills, estate division and family property disputes. Strategy considers future living arrangements and long-term feasibility alongside legal outcomes.

Wealth succession is often better planned before a dispute arises. Wills, gifts, review of co-ownership, asset arrangements and family agreements can reduce uncertainty. When disputes have already arisen, we clarify rights through evidence, negotiation, mediation or litigation. The best solution is not always the most adversarial one; it is an arrangement that protects essential rights and can work in practice.
