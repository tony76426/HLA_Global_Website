WHAT IS LAWAI

What Is LawAI?

LawAI helps users organize their questions, facts, documents and possible approaches before bringing matters requiring professional judgment to a lawyer.

Organizing information can make consultations more efficient, but it cannot guarantee that the information provided is complete or that any conclusion is correct. A lawyer must verify the applicable law, evaluate evidence and develop a strategy based on the circumstances of each case.

THE LAWYER LAYER

AI Helps Organize.
People Make the Judgment.

Digital tools can highlight missing information, but they cannot independently establish the authenticity or context of every document or understand a client’s objectives. A lawyer reviews the facts, legal rules, deadlines, evidence and available procedures, then explains the trade-offs between possible courses of action.
