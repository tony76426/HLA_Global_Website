Unpaid Child Support: Notices and Enforcement

Child support may arise from a private agreement, court mediation, settlement or judgment, each with different enforceability. A message promising payment alone does not authorize wage attachment. Review the amount, commencement date, due dates, conditions and recipient. Key Takeaways

  • Identify the document that establishes support obligations
  • Reconstruct unpaid installments month by month
  • A demand notice should be precise, not threatening

Identify the document that establishes support obligations

Child support may arise from a private agreement, court mediation, settlement or judgment, each with different enforceability. A message promising payment alone does not authorize wage attachment.

Review the amount, commencement date, due dates, conditions and recipient. Determine whether the document sets out a definite monthly payment or only a general obligation to meet living costs; some claims require a court process before an enforceable title is obtained.

Reconstruct unpaid installments month by month

For each installment list the due date, amount owed, actual receipt and outstanding balance. Record tuition, insurance or other direct payments separately until their treatment under the original obligation is confirmed.

Retain bank statements, transfer descriptions and complete conversations, including any agreed extension or change. A verifiable month-by-month schedule is more useful than an unsupported total.

A demand notice should be precise, not threatening

State the overdue months, amount, payment method and reasonable response deadline; keep evidence of delivery. A demand helps define the dispute but neither creates a court judgment nor permits immediate seizure merely because a certified letter was sent.

Communications should focus on the child's needs rather than pressure transmitted through the child. If a partial payment plan is proposed, record clearly what happens to existing arrears.

Without an enforceable title, establish the claim first

A private arrangement is not always directly enforceable. Family mediation, an application or litigation may be necessary to determine the payment obligation and obtain a suitable enforcement title.

Claims for past advances and future periodic support can require different treatment. In mediation, specify the start, each due date, past arrears and exceptional expenses, and assess whether a guarantee or installment plan is realistically workable.

Enforcement requires a title and a lawful procedure

With a qualifying enforceable title, apply to the competent enforcement court and provide the required documents and debtor information.

Wage, account or asset leads can inform the choice of assets, but a claimant cannot order an employer or bank to deduct payments independently. Special family-law enforcement rules, the debtor's necessary living expenses, available assets and competing claims affect what can actually be recovered; full immediate recovery is never guaranteed.

Reduced income does not automatically cancel the duty

Unemployment or major financial changes may justify considering a lawful adjustment to support, but one party's message saying payment is impossible does not automatically invalidate an existing agreement or order.

The recipient should also document genuine changes when exploring a sustainable plan. Any new arrangement must address the effective period, arrears and consistency with the previous court document.

Track the child's needs alongside the legal process

Maintain a recurring payment ledger, the child's education and medical expenses, and a secure evidence folder. Urgent needs may call for advice on provisional family measures or social assistance in parallel with recovery.

Enforcement implements a proven right; it cannot create assets where none exist. Balance evidence, enforceability, costs and time, and do not overlook statutory limits or court deadlines while negotiating.

Documents to prepare before consultation

Bring the full agreement, mediation record or judgment; a monthly arrears and receipts schedule; bank statements and messages; debtor identification and known asset leads; and records of the child's urgent expenses. Mark missing items and possible sources, preserving original files.

Frequently asked questions about collection

Can a divorce agreement be used to attach wages immediately?

Only if its content and legal status qualify as an enforceable title; a private agreement differs from court mediation or judgment.

Can you enforce a partly paid obligation?

First calculate remaining amounts by installment and disclose any agreement on reduction, postponement or setoff, then assess enforcement within the title's scope.
