PROFILE / APPROACH

About Cheng Tien-Hao

Attorney Cheng Tien-Hao graduated from the Department of Law at National Taipei University and currently practices as an associate attorney at HLA Global.

He regards criminal defense not only as a profession but also as a responsibility to due process and fundamental rights. He carefully reviews facts, evidence and procedural steps, helping clients understand their position and prepare a suitable response.

Criminal investigations and trials can involve demanding deadlines. Through clear communication and careful legal work, he seeks to ensure that every client’s account receives a fair and thorough hearing.
