The duty of support survives divorce

Under Article 1116-2 of Taiwan's Civil Code, a parent's duty to maintain a minor child is unaffected by annulment or divorce. Residence, parental authority and visitation do not by themselves end that duty.

Article 1119 requires consideration of the child's needs and the responsible person's resources and circumstances. Examine assets, reliable income, other necessary obligations and actual care; unemployment claims alone do not settle the question.

Start with the child's actual budget

List food, housing, transport, clothing, education, medical care and necessary supervision with their payment cycles. Spread annual or semester expenses into monthly averages but retain original receipts.

Use a reasonable method to apportion shared household expenses rather than charging the whole family bill to one child. Separate past, ongoing and proposed spending; identify disputed high-cost activities so that every claimed amount is linked to a real need.

Statistics do not replace individual evidence

Local living-cost and household expenditure statistics may help frame negotiations, but a child's age, health, district, home and care arrangements remain decisive facts.

Cite the year, area and source of any statistics; a search-result number is not an automatic court standard. For medical treatment, rehabilitation or special education, document needs, plans, costs and other benefits, then decide who pays, when and how to verify them.

Consider both financial and practical care

Parents' contributions need not be 50:50 when their resources differ. The resident parent may satisfy part of the obligation through everyday care, transport and direct expenses; the other parent may additionally share agreed exceptional costs.

A simple ratio of salaries is only a starting point. Review income stability, other dependants and care responsibilities; record unknown information as a request for evidence rather than treating estimates as established income.

An example calculation is not a legal ruling

For illustration only, assume verified needs of NT$24,000 per month and a provisional 60/40 division: NT$14,400 and NT$9,600 respectively. Direct payments and practical care must be accounted for without double counting.

This is neither a published court rate nor advice for a particular family. Changes to medical needs, housing or income require revisiting the assumptions; show the calculation so the parties can evaluate it against the child's interests.

Payment terms determine enforceability

An agreement should state the payment date, account, commencement month, notification and reconciliation for exceptional expenses, and treatment of duplicate payments. Saying only 'pay as needed' leaves scope and verification uncertain.

Mark bank transfers with a clear purpose. Major changes in prices, schooling or income may call for review under Civil Code Article 1121 and related rules, but an existing duty should not be unilaterally suspended without checking the agreement or order and lawful procedure.

Separate arrears, reimbursement and future support

Overdue maintenance, past expenses advanced by one parent and future periodic payments may involve different legal grounds and procedures. Prepare a month-by-month schedule of amounts due, received and outstanding, attaching transfer evidence and flagging disputed months.

Support and contact arrangements are separate matters: a child should not become leverage for payment. If basic living or schooling is at risk, seek advice promptly on provisional orders or other appropriate relief.

Documents for consultation

Bring a monthly budget and receipts, school and medical invoices, records of both parents' incomes and obligations, the actual care schedule, and any agreement, judgment or payment history. If information is missing, identify what is missing and where it might be obtained. Keep original records.

Frequently asked questions

Must parents always pay half each?

No: the child's needs, each parent's capacity and practical caregiving all matter.

Can support be stopped if contact is refused?

No automatic link exists between support and visitation. Resolve contact disputes separately and review any existing payment order through proper procedures.
