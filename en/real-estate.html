PREPARE & UNDERSTAND

What should you prepare before a real-estate consultation?

What must be distinguished in co-owned or inherited property?

Check registered owners, ownership shares, inheritance relationships and current use, then distinguish management, sale and partition objectives. Where estate division is involved, connect inheritance documents with co-ownership relationships rather than relying only on share percentages.

  • Partitioning Co-Owned Land: Available Options ↗
  • Inherited Real Estate: Registration and Partition ↗

How should you prepare for a property sale or lease dispute?

Prepare land registry transcripts, contracts and exhibits, payments, repairs, notices and handover records. Because property transactions and recovery have different performance milestones, counsel can compare negotiation, litigation and enforcement based on documents and objectives.

PRACTICE / HLA

View legal title, transaction documents and actual occupation together.

Land and real-estate matters often involve long timelines, multiple stakeholders, complex documents and substantial asset values. Registered title, actual use, co-ownership, transaction terms, land and building rights, possession and historic documents may all influence outcomes. HLA draws on experience in land law, partition of co-owned property and property disputes to analyze rights and procedure together.

We assist with sales, leases, partition, ownership and possession, recovery of property, neighboring property damage, land use, condominium matters and other real-estate disputes. For high-value or multiply owned assets, we assess not only legal title but partition options, use and income, sale of assets and enforceability, so clients understand practical alternatives to litigation.

Key services

  • Land and building ownership disputes
  • Partition of co-owned property and co-ownership issues
  • Property sales, defects and purchase price disputes
  • Leases, eviction, recovery and possession
  • Condominiums, buildings and use rights
  • Real-estate enforcement and asset dispositions

Land & real-estate FAQ
