PREPARE & UNDERSTAND

What should you prepare before an employment-law consultation?

What records matter in a labor dispute?

Gather employment agreements, workplace rules, pay and attendance records, job changes and significant communications, noting dates. Counsel compares actual conditions with the disputed issues; contract wording alone is rarely the complete picture.

  • Employment Records from Hiring to Departure ↗

How should handovers and confidential information be handled together?

Review job authority, accessible information, confidentiality terms and handover lists. Record returns, transfers and subsequent use. Where technology or trade secrets are involved, review intellectual property ownership and protective measures as well.

PRACTICE / HLA

Turn employment-law obligations into workable management systems.

Employment relationships are among the most routine—and risk-prone—aspects of business. Recruitment, probation, hours, wages, bonuses, transfers, appraisals, confidentiality, non-compete obligations, redundancy and departure can all have legal consequences. HLA reviews employment contracts, workplace rules and HR procedures, and assists with negotiation, mediation and litigation when disputes arise.

For employees, we help clarify employment status, pay, overtime, severance, dismissal, occupational injury and other rights. Employment cases often depend on accumulated records and actual practice, so we closely compare attendance, wages, duties, management records, communications and policies. For employers, sound systems provide a clear basis for management decisions, not just more paperwork.

Key services

  • Employment contracts and workplace rules
  • Working hours, wages, bonuses and overtime pay
  • Transfers, evaluations, redundancy and dismissal
  • Confidentiality, non-compete clauses and trade secrets
  • Occupational injury and labor disputes
  • Labor mediation and employment litigation

04 / FAQ

Employment & HR FAQ

Should workplace rules and employment contracts be reviewed together?＋

Yes. Compare both against actual working conditions to avoid gaps between written policies and practice.

What should I gather first in a labor dispute?＋

Prepare employment documents, payroll records, communications and a timeline of relevant events.
