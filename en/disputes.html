PREPARE & UNDERSTAND

What should you prepare before a civil dispute consultation?

For unpaid debts or breaches, connect each claim to evidence

Gather contracts, messages, payment and delivery records. Separate completed obligations, outstanding obligations and disputed items. Counsel can then assess claims, deadlines and any preservation needs so negotiation and litigation rely on the same factual record.

How can a judgment or settlement be enforced?

Dispute strategy must consider whether the result can be realized. Gather the other party’s details, known asset information and available documents. Distinguish matters needing a judgment from those with enforceable instruments or room to negotiate, then set priorities.

PRACTICE / HLA

The question is not just whether to sue, but which route best resolves the dispute.

Civil and commercial disputes are a core part of HLA’s practice: contracts, loans, negotiable instruments, damages, debt claims, partnerships, sales, construction, services, torts and property disputes. Before filing suit, preserve evidence, check limitation periods and deadlines, and assess asset preservation, negotiation options and the other party’s ability to perform.

HLA emphasizes pre-litigation planning. We build a complete chronology, distinguish provable facts from gaps and anticipate possible defenses before choosing demand letters, provisional attachment or injunctions, mediation, litigation or other procedures. In court, we focus on issues, evidence chains and consistent pleadings. If settlement is possible, we also consider payment terms, security, default consequences and enforcement—not merely a signed agreement.

  • Loans, negotiable instruments, debt recovery and restructuring
  • Damages and tort liability
  • Provisional attachment, injunctions and preservation
  • Mediation, negotiation, litigation and compulsory enforcement
