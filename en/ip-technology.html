PREPARE & UNDERSTAND

What should you prepare before an IP and technology consultation?

Which rights should you check before launching a product?

Identify the sources of images, code, data and outsourced work, along with contracts, licences and permitted uses. Compare these with product functions and public terms, and maintain traceable records of data flows, internal access and confidentiality controls.

  • Before Product Launch: Data, Licenses and Platform Terms ↗

How should licensing information be organized when using generative AI?

Keep source inputs, tool terms, intended outputs and human-editing records separate. Identify where the data came from and how it will be used before assessing licensing and confidentiality. For employee or outsourced work, also review authority and contracts.

PRACTICE / HLA

Make innovation usable while protecting valuable assets.

Corporate assets increasingly consist of brands, software, data, content, technology and trade secrets. HLA advises on copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, software and technology service agreements, data use and personal-data protection, translating legal requirements into cooperation terms, internal processes and dispute strategies.

As AI, SaaS, platforms and digital content evolve, conventional contracts may not adequately address data sources, model outputs, licensing scope, third-party content, security or allocation of responsibility. HLA first understands the product workflow and business model before designing fit-for-purpose legal arrangements. For IP and technology disputes, we also assess evidence preservation, scope of infringement, business impact and procedural options.

  • SaaS, software and technology service contracts
