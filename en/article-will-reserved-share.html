Wills and Reserved Shares under Taiwan Law

Taiwan's Civil Code recognises different forms of wills, each with distinct rules for writing, witnessing, signatures and procedure. An online template does not ensure legal capacity or proper execution. Decide which form will be used, check dates and witnesses, and keep suitable evidence where language, literacy, health or communication may be questioned. Notarisation serves a purpose but does not automatically eliminate property disputes or reserved-share claims.

  • A will must meet statutory formalities

A will must meet statutory formalities

Taiwan's Civil Code recognises different forms of wills, each with distinct rules for writing, witnessing, signatures and procedure. An online template does not ensure legal capacity or proper execution.

Decide which form will be used, check dates and witnesses, and keep suitable evidence where language, literacy, health or communication may be questioned. Notarisation serves a purpose but does not automatically eliminate property disputes or reserved-share claims.

Inventory the property you actually own

List real property, deposits, investments, company interests and other assets, together with ownership shares, debts and contractual limits. Property used by a person may not be wholly theirs to dispose of.

Insurance, trusts and business interests can follow separate arrangements. Identify items precisely rather than as 'the old house', and revisit the plan after asset sales, replacements or debt changes. An attached list is not automatically valid as a will unless formal requirements are met.

Statutory inheritance shares differ from reserved portions

An intestate share concerns the allocation under succession law, whereas a reserved portion protects certain heirs from arrangements made by will and related dispositions. Articles 1187 and 1223 of Taiwan's Civil Code are relevant.

The calculation may involve estate value, debts, lifetime gifts and particular statutory rules. Determine which heirs can claim, whether the minimum was infringed and the appropriate relief using full family and asset records; ignoring mandatory protection can increase later disputes.

An announced amendment is not automatically in force

At this article's stated update date of 8 October 2026, the amendment to Civil Code Article 1223 announced on 17 August 2026 was scheduled to take effect six months after promulgation and was not yet effective. A legal database may show new text before its effective date.

Check the commencement notes, previous text and transitional provisions; the applicable rule depends on the date inheritance begins. Never apply an amendment prematurely to determine an heir's rights.

Check whether the intended distribution can work

Different aims—housing for a family member, care for a dependent, preserving a business or avoiding co-ownership—require different tools. Splitting one property between many heirs can create long-term management conflict; allocating it to one heir with compensation requires realistic cash flow.

Map each asset to the intended recipient, obligations, deadlines and alternatives, while considering minors or vulnerable beneficiaries. Wills, trusts, insurance and lifetime transfers differ in legal and tax consequences.

Plan custody, execution and version control

Even a valid will can be difficult to implement if no one knows where it is or inconsistent versions exist. Arrange secure storage, appropriate contacts and a capable executor where relevant.

Protect private family and account details. When revising, check revocation, conflicts and the form required for a new valid instrument; changing a photocopy does not necessarily amend a will. Reassess after major family, marriage or asset changes.

If a dispute arises, preserve records and deadlines

After inheritance opens, disagreements may concern authenticity, formalities, capacity, reserved shares or what property the will covers. Preserve the original instrument, creation records and relevant messages; not every disagreement means the entire will is invalid.

Identify the specific claim, applicable procedure and limitation period. Consultation should include family relationships, an asset inventory, all versions of the will and notices, whether seeking to uphold or challenge the arrangement.

Documents for an inheritance-planning consultation

Bring family and inheritance relationships; property, co-ownership percentages and debts; current and former wills; planning objectives and compensation ability; and ideas for custody and an executor. Record unavailable information and preserve originals.

Frequently asked questions about wills

Does a will completely displace statutory succession shares?

It can provide for distribution but must satisfy formalities and restrictions such as reserved portions; the estate and debts matter too.

May the newest text shown in a statute database be used immediately?

Not necessarily: confirm its effective date and transitional rules. The August 2026 amendment to Article 1223 was not yet in force on the article's October 2026 update date.
